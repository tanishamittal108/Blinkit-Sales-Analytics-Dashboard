# Blinkit-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard

# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using Power BI to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, outlet distribution, product categories, and customer ratings. The dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue trends, outlet performance, and product-level sales metrics to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit's retail sales data and transforming raw business data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze outlet-wise revenue generation
- Compare product category performance
- Track customer ratings and item visibility
- Evaluate outlet size and location impact on sales
- Identify business growth trends over time

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. Without proper analysis, identifying high-performing outlets, profitable product categories, and customer preferences becomes challenging.

This dashboard helps answer key business questions:

- Which outlet types generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute most to total sales?
- How does outlet size impact sales performance?
- Which outlet locations are most profitable?
- How have sales trends evolved over the years?

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Outlet Location Analysis
- Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales revenue.
- Tier 1 outlets showed comparatively lower revenue contribution.

### Product Performance
- Fruits and Snacks emerged as top-performing categories.
- Seafood and Breakfast categories contributed the least revenue.

### Outlet Size Impact
- Medium-sized outlets generated the highest sales.
- Smaller outlets showed lower overall revenue generation.

### Customer Satisfaction
- Average rating remained close to 4.0, indicating generally positive customer feedback.
